Who We Are

Our Mission

"To foster a community of USD students who are prepared to lead in a future shaped by artificial intelligence, grounded in a commitment to the ethical and responsible use of AI in business."

AI Society Constitution

"We believe AI is going to show up in every job, not only the technical ones."

That's the whole reason this org exists. Finance, marketing, consulting, operations — these fields are already changing. Most business programs haven't caught up yet, and most students are graduating without ever really engaging with AI in a serious way.

We're not a fan club. We don't think AI is the answer to everything. We do think that not understanding it is a real liability, and that's what we're here to address.

That means learning how the tools work, but also learning where they fail: the bias, the hallucinations, the open questions around IP and ethics. The students who do well in this environment won't be the ones who hype it. They'll be the ones who think clearly about it.

01

Accessible to everyone

No CS background needed. If you're a business student curious about AI, you belong here.

02

Build and ship

Build the prototype, then argue about it. We learn by making real things, not sitting through decks.

03

Honest about AI's limits

Bias, hallucinations, IP concerns. We talk about all of it. Being informed means knowing where these tools fall short, not just where they work.

04

Show up for each other

We're a team. The relationships built here matter as much as anything else you'll take from it.

How we started

It started in a classroom.

The AI Society grew out of Dr. Alan Lerchbacker's MGMT 300 class in spring 2026. A group of his students wanted a place at USD to actually learn AI instead of just reading headlines about it, so they started one.

By May, there was a board. The rest is history still being made.

Community Collaboration

"The best learning happens alongside people actually doing the work."

We're looking for companies, professionals, and organizations who want to connect with a new generation of business students. Students who take AI seriously and think critically about it.
