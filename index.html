Who We Are

Our Mission

AI Society Constitution

"To foster a community of USD students who are prepared to lead in a future shaped by artificial intelligence, grounded in a commitment to the ethical and responsible use of AI in business."

"We believe AI is going to show up in every job, not only the technical ones."

That's the whole reason this org exists. Finance, marketing, consulting, operations — these fields are already changing. Most business programs haven't caught up, and most students are graduating without ever seriously engaging with AI.

We're not a fan club. We don't think AI is the answer to everything. We do think that not understanding it is a real liability, and that's what we're here to address.

That means learning how the tools work, but also learning where they fail: the bias, the hallucinations, the open questions around IP and ethics. The students who do well in this environment won't be the ones who hype it. They'll be the ones who think clearly about it.

01

Accessible to everyone

No CS background needed. If you're a business student curious about AI, you belong here.

02

Build and ship

Build the prototype, then argue about it. We learn by making real things, not sitting through decks.

03

Honest about AI's limits

Bias, hallucinations, IP concerns. We talk about all of it. Being informed means knowing where these tools fall short, not just where they shine.

04

Show up for each other

We're a team. The relationships you build here matter as much as anything else you'll take from it.
